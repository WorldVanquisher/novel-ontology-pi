Speaker attribution — whose line is it

status: v1 patch (skeleton) repo: src/autostep/patches/speaker-attribution.ts model: Jev · choice

The last structural patch: it takes the dialogue units from sector 03 and decides which character each line belongs to — and, as a sub-function, who the line is addressed to. Its output — turns with speakers — is the skeleton extraction (08) hangs events on.

InputsDialogue units (03) + characters present (05) + last speaker + speech-tag sentences
OutputsTurns: { speakerId, addresseeId, sentenceIds } per unit → SPEAKS_TO / ADDRESSES material for extraction
Depends on03 Dialogue units, 05 Presence
Feeds into08 Extraction, 12 Simulation (voice evidence)

Decision questions

dialogue_speaker · choice — Who speaks this dialogue unit? Options: the characters present + narrator + unknown. Criteria carry the craft: speech style, forms of address, the previous turn's speaker (dialogue usually alternates), and who the narration focuses on.
dialogue_addressee · choice candidate — Who is being spoken to? Options: present characters + group + none. Needed for ADDRESSES edges in multi-party scenes.

Design details

  • Choice fits exactly: a turn has one speaker — this is the one patch where Jev's single-pick primitive is precisely the shape of the problem.
  • "Unknown" is a first-class answer: an unresolved speaker escalates rather than guessing — a wrong speaker poisons voice learning in simulation; an unknown merely waits.
  • Evidence is stored: the winning probability rides with the turn, so the merge gate can sort its review by shakiest attributions first.

Implementation

  • src/autostep/patches/speaker-attribution.ts — one choice per unit from ctx.accepted.dialogueUnits; unresolved units become escalations.
  • Runs last in the v1 registry — it consumes the most context of any patch.

Open questions — speaker attribution

  • First-person narration.In I-novels the narrator IS a character. Does "narrator" collapse into that character once known, series-wide?
  • Interruptions and overlaps.Two characters finish each other's sentences; a line is cut off and resumed. Turn model may need "interrupted" and "resumed" links.
  • Speakers not yet in the graph.A voice from off-page whose name sector 04 hasn't proposed yet — attribute to a placeholder and reconcile at the gate?
  • Addressee in group scenes.Speaking to a room vs. to one person in it — how much does extraction actually gain from addressee edges? Decide before promoting the candidate question.
