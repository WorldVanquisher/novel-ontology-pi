Presence detector — who is actually in the scene

status: v1 patch (skeleton) repo: src/autostep/patches/presence-detector.ts model: Jev · noul × candidates

The distinction this sector protects is small and crucial: a character can be mentioned in a scene — talked about, remembered, phoned — without being present. Only presence feeds simulation state. Confuse the two, and characters start knowing things they never witnessed.

InputsOne scene span + candidate characters (from 04's scans + previous-scene carryover)
OutputscharactersPresent / charactersMentioned per scene → PRESENT_IN / MENTIONED_IN proposals

Decision questions

is_present_<candidate> · noul — Is this character physically present and able to act/speak in this scene (not merely mentioned, remembered, or spoken about)? Asked once per candidate — because choice returns a single pick, a cast of five can never be one question.

Design details

  • Candidates are cheap, questions are spent wisely: the deterministic name scan (04) plus previous-scene carryover builds the candidate list; Jev only judges those names.
  • Mentioned is recorded, not discarded: a MENTIONED_IN edge is real knowledge — it tells simulation who is being talked about, and it often foreshadows an entrance.
  • Highest gates in the registry: presence feeds character state directly, so this patch accepts at 0.85 and escalates eagerly.

Implementation

  • src/autostep/patches/presence-detector.ts — one noul per candidate in ctx.accepted.presenceCandidates; splits answers into charactersPresent / charactersMentioned.

Open questions — presence

  • Off-screen presence.On the phone, behind the door, watching through a camera — present, mentioned, or a third state (present_remote)? Simulation cares: they can hear, but can't act.
  • Entrances and exits mid-scene.Uiharu enters at s0004. Is presence per-scene, or per-sentence-range inside the scene? Scene B exists in the demo because sector 02 split there — when it doesn't split, presence needs ranges.
  • Groups and crowds."The class", "the patrol" — collective presence as one pseudo-entity, or expanded to members?
  • Non-person presences.Corpses, portraits, recordings, an AI on a screen — do they get PRESENT_IN with a qualifier?
